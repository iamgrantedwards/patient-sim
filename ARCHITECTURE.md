# Architecture

The Python dispatcher validates the single assessment destination and starts a dedicated local LiveKit Agents worker. After registration, it dispatches one job into a unique Cloud room. The worker prepares recording and a separate STT → text LLM → TTS session, then dials through a Twilio Elastic SIP trunk using one fixed caller number. Deepgram Flux, GPT-4.1-mini and Cartesia Sonic 3.6 use LiveKit Inference to keep provider configuration small while retaining the required pipeline. Flux supplies turn detection; VAD handles interruptions. Preemptive generation is off to avoid replying to incomplete turns; noise cancellation and automatic gain control are off to preserve the evaluated audio. These are engineering tradeoffs, not measured superiority claims: the planned configuration comparison and audio-aligned latency measurements are deferred. The synthetic patient waits for the greeting and follows a versioned scenario objective without seeing evaluator traps. One-call locks, confirmation, single-job admission, child-failure reporting, cleanup and duration/turn limits bound failures. The observed native SIGSEGV cause remains unresolved; containment is not a native-library fix.

The worker journals events incrementally, reconciles committed dialogue without duplicating partial speech, preserves SDK stereo OGG before temporary cleanup and verifies decoding. Code/scenario/model/prompt provenance stays with each call; spoken confirmations and cross-call consistency are distinct from verified backend outcomes. A loopback-only FastAPI console serves local files with bundled HTML/CSS/JavaScript and native playback. It defaults to read-only; explicit flags enable calls, atomic revisioned human reviews or paid AI transcript assessments. The separate GPT-4.1 judge uses LiveKit Inference, a five-dimension office-agent rubric plus patient/turn-taking/ending text checks and one call's scenario ID, transcript and allowlisted capture/termination metadata; it receives neither audio nor the Markdown test strategy. Schema/exact-quote checks and deterministic score arithmetic constrain its output, but semantic grades remain uncalibrated suggestions, not audio or backend verification. Assessments and human judgments persist separately with evidence fingerprints; neither rewrites original recordings. No database, public hosting or reviewer access to our credentials is required to inspect deliberately published evidence. Offline tests verify our safeguards, not real voice quality. Ten candidate pairs are captured; human listening and public-release acceptance remain in the submission checklist.
