Quick guide

Run a simulated conversation, listen back, and keep your observations with the call.

Start here

  1. Choose a call. Select a card or list row in Calls. Its recording, transcript and reviews appear below.
  2. Listen back. Play the recording. Open Conversation transcript when you need the text.
  3. Keep what you learn. Call cards show the current AI score or assessment status and a separate human-review indicator. “Notes saved” means listening has not been confirmed. “Outdated” means the saved result needs another look; a score marked “prior” is historical. Use AI assessment for analysis, or Review to save your observations.

To start a new conversation, use Run a test call. Your simulated patient does the speaking.

Find a call and arrange your workspace
  • Use the card and list icons to change the Calls layout. Selecting either view keeps the same call selected.
  • Open the magnifying glass beside the call count to search by scenario or call ID, or filter by review and file status. Clear filters restores the full list.
  • Use the refresh icon in the top bar to reload saved calls. Each attempt has its own record, including failed or incomplete attempts.
  • The sun or moon icon changes the theme. The light bulb enables short hints: hover, focus or tap a question mark. The book opens this guide.

Audio and Transcript badges show which files are available. They do not mean the conversation has been reviewed.

Run a new call and follow it live
  1. Choose a Scenario and expand Scenario details to read the patient’s goal.
  2. Select Review & call. Check the destination, caller number and time limit, then acknowledge the confirmation and choose Place one call.
  3. The Live transcript opens automatically. Watch the connection status and follow the conversation; text appears as turns are captured.
  4. After the call finishes saving, select its record in Calls to listen back.

Collapse Live transcript whenever you like; it closes automatically when the call finishes. A pause in text does not necessarily mean the call has stopped. Live text follows new turns automatically. Scroll up to read earlier turns; select Latest turns ↓ to resume following. The two speakers have different backgrounds.

Only one call runs at a time, to the configured destination. If Run a test call is absent, calling is not enabled in this workspace.

Listen and use the transcript

Under Conversation, use the recording’s play, pause and seek controls. Audio never plays automatically, and selecting another call stops the previous recording.

Open Conversation transcript to follow the speakers. It starts closed for each selected call. Download .txt saves the text to your device.

Speech recognition can mishear names or words. Compare important quotes with the audio. Partial speech remains visible; it may indicate an interrupted turn.

Turn numbers identify the order of speech, not elapsed time. Audio seek links are available only when timing was measured. If audio is missing, the transcript alone cannot establish how the conversation sounded or ended.

Save a listening review
  1. Open Review below the recording. Enter your name and choose a Conversation result: Needs recheck, Usable conversation or Incomplete attempt.
  2. Add a short note if useful. In Detailed checklist (optional), mark only what you checked: OK or Issue. Leave the rest Not assessed; each Issue needs an observation.
  3. For Usable conversation, listen to the full recording, check the transcript, select the listening confirmation and mark Complete evidence as OK in the checklist.
  4. Select Save review, or Save revision when updating a saved review. Wait for the saved revision message before leaving the call.

You can save a draft as Needs recheck without confirming listening. Playback does not mark a call reviewed. Saved revisions remain available in Review; changed source files trigger a recheck.

Add AI summary copies a current saved assessment into your notes as an AI draft. Edit it as needed, then save. It does not run an assessment or fill in your checklist or listening confirmation.

If Review is read-only, review saving has not been enabled for this workspace.

Use an AI assessment

Open AI assessment below the recording and select Assess transcript. This sends the selected call’s transcript, scenario identifier and capture details to LiveKit Inference and uses account credits. It does not place a phone call.

The analysis covers request handling, consistency, clarification, supported claims and next steps. Expand each dimension for its reasoning and supporting quotes. Select a quoted turn to open it in the transcript; audio seeks there only when timing is available.

Scores are provisional: 2 means no material issue observed, 1 a minor gap, 0 a substantial issue, and N/A not assessable. A score out of 100 appears only when at least three of the five dimensions can be assessed.

Call quality covers the seven review topics separately from the score. Expand a row for its evidence and suggested next step. Ending opens expanded: “Caller ended” records who hung up, not whether the final words finished playing. AI checks the patient’s behavior, turn-taking concerns and final exchange in text. Transcript accuracy, pacing and audio clarity show Needs listening. These checks do not fill in your listening review.

Observations include uncertainty, suggested improvements and a next test. Check them against the recording. The AI reads text only: it cannot judge pronunciation, audio quality or whether an appointment was actually saved. Its suggestions do not change the agent or run another test.

A finished conversation with both speakers and a recording is required. Read-only mode lets you view saved results. When evidence or assessment settings change, the old score is withheld; Reassess transcript requests a new analysis. Reopening a current result does not generate another assessment.

Stop a call or handle a problem

Stop call ends the active call and keeps whatever was captured. The record distinguishes an operator stop from a natural ending; unfinished files may be incomplete.

Refreshing or closing the browser does not stop a call. Reopen the workspace and check its status before trying again.

If Stop / recover appears, use it to finish cleanup. Another call stays blocked until cleanup is confirmed. There is no automatic redial.

If calls or reviews fail to load, check that the local app is still running, then refresh. A missing recording or transcript may mean the call is still saving or ended early; check the call’s status and notice before retrying.

If saving a review fails, keep your notes and check the saved revision before trying again. If an AI assessment fails, reopen the call to check for a saved result before choosing Retry assessment.

Understand the call details tabs
Conversation
The selected call’s recording, transcript, AI assessment and listening review.
Provenance
The models, settings, code version and prompt fingerprint saved with this call. Later changes do not rewrite them. Cloud evidence, when present, provides a dated view of that session.
Controls & data
The current workspace mode, call limits, storage locations and external services.
Know where your data goes

Call files, saved reviews and AI assessments are stored on the computer running the app. Refreshing the page does not erase them. Reviews and assessments are separate from the original recording and transcript.

Theme, layout and learning preferences belong to your browser. A downloaded transcript is a separate copy; it does not include your saved review or AI assessment.

Calls use LiveKit, Twilio and speech and language services. AI assessment sends transcript text through LiveKit Inference, without the recording or your review notes. A local page does not mean all processing happens on your computer.

Use synthetic patient details. Check audio and text for personal information before sharing; the app does not automatically redact them.
