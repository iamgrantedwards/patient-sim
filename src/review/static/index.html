Quick guide

Run a call at the top. Review what happened below.

Your first test

  1. Choose a scenario. It gives our simulated patient a goal. You do not speak into the call.
  2. Review & call. Check the fixed destination, then confirm one call. Opening the page or this guide never dials.
  3. Watch the status. Follow connection and live text. Audio playback is available after the call.
  4. Open saved evidence. After finalization, the call appears in the list. Select it to listen and compare the transcript.

If call controls are absent, this console is in read-only mode.

What do the three tabs mean?
Conversation
The selected call’s original audio and transcript. Partial speech stays visible.
Provenance
The models, settings, code revision and prompt fingerprint recorded for that call. Later changes do not rewrite them.
Controls & data
The current console mode, call limits and where files are stored.
What should I test next?

Aim for one complete conversation first. Listen all the way through and check:

  • Both voices are audible.
  • Replies make sense and turns do not repeatedly cut each other off.
  • The transcript matches the audio; incomplete speech is identified.
  • The conversation ends cleanly. A polite refusal can be a valid outcome.

After a complete, reviewed call, try another scenario or compare configurations. Change one thing at a time so you can trace what improved.

How do I stop or recover a call?

Stop call ends the active call and preserves available evidence. An operator stop is recorded separately from a natural ending, and unfinished audio may be incomplete.

Refreshing or closing the browser does not stop a call. Reopen the console and use Stop. If status is lost, do not assume the call ended.

Stop / recover is offered when cleanup is uncertain. Another call stays blocked until cleanup is confirmed. Do not delete recovery files to bypass it; there is no automatic redial.

What counts as usable evidence?

An audio/transcript pair means both files exist. It does not establish voice quality or a complete conversation. Playing audio does not update the recorded human-review status.

Check quotes against the audio. Turn numbers are not timestamps. A recording fingerprint identifies file contents; it is not independent proof of correctness. A spoken booking confirmation is a claim until independently verified.

What are the privacy and AI limits?

Use synthetic patient facts and the designated assessment line. This tool evaluates an agent; it does not deliver patient care or make clinical decisions.

Remote responses may contain personal information. Review audio and text before publishing. Calls involve LiveKit, Twilio and inference providers; a local viewer does not mean all call data stays on-device.

Governance controls are documented, not certified. Provider retention, access and other obligations need separate review before use beyond this assessment. The app does not claim HIPAA compliance.
